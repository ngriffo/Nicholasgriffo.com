ABOUT FRAME 01

Film is decisions made one frame at a time. AI is the same decisions made at scale.

I'm a filmmaker, writer, and creative technologist. Two screenplays in development, written the old way, because the stories demand it. AI isn't making movies, it's a toolkit, and a powerful one at that.

I get the panic. They're scanning actors' faces and soon all cinema will be re-engineered slop. It isn't true. Every generation of filmmakers gets a new tool and the same conversation follows. The camera was going to kill painting. Sound was going to kill cinema. CGI was going to kill practical effects. None of it happened. What happened instead was that the best artists figured out what the new thing made possible and ran toward it. That's what I'm doing.

Six years building physical sets for Google, Meta, Coachella, and more than a dozen film productions, including a 16mm feature. The last year evaluating and improving generative AI models for a leading AI lab. I know what a set needs to feel real before the camera rolls, and I know what a generated frame has to feel like before it goes anywhere.

AI isn't coming for the storytellers. It's coming for the parts of production that were always in the way of the story. That's good news. That's actually great news.

I write stories a model couldn't invent on its own. Built specifically to live in the gap AI just opened, too strange for a traditional budget and too human to come from anywhere but a person.
