5. From Set to Model

What carries over
On setIn a generative pipeline
Production designMy job is deciding what’s in the frame and why. Same job with a model, except this crew takes everything literally.
ContinuityOn set I’m the one who notices the coffee cup moved between takes. Generated video moves the coffee cup constantly. Hands, too.
Working to a shot listI write out what the shot needs before I generate anything. Saves a lot of rerolls.
Budget and sourcingEvery job has a number. I can tell you which parts the tools can carry and which parts still need a person.
Shooting on filmOn 16mm you get a couple of takes. You learn to know what you want before you spend it.

6. Personal Projects

3 builds
6.1

Ultralight Base

A free reference site for ultralight backpacking

Designed, built, and maintained independently. A base-weight calculator, a database of 1,083 items across 266 brands, a permit calendar, and guides to 65 trails, 45 of them permit-controlled. Works offline once saved to a phone.

The data is kept current by an automated monthly pipeline. It checks permit windows, prices, and weights against official and manufacturer sources, rebuilds the trail pages, tests the site, and submits every change for review before it goes live.

6.2

Today’s News

Daily since October 7, 2026

A daily newspaper, researched, written, laid out, and printed at home each morning by a system of scheduled AI agents, designed and directed by Nicholas. Standing editorial rules set the sections, the sourcing standard, and the length. A layout engine sets the paper in columns with archival pictures and holds weekday editions to four pages. A print queue on a home computer sends it to the printer.

One day’s issue: November 6, 2026 (PDF, 4 pages).

6.3

E-Ink Alarm Clock

In progress

A bedside clock to replace the iPhone alarm. Most bedside clocks light up the room all night, even dimmed. This one uses an e-paper screen, which shows the time without giving off any light. One press of the button turns on two amber lights for reading it in the dark. Alarms come straight from the phone’s own Clock app, so there is no separate app to install or keep in sync.

A small, rounded raw-aluminum shell with a curved back, a 2.13-inch e-paper screen, two amber LEDs, a single push button, and a small speaker.
