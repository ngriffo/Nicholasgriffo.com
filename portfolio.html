1.Summary

1.1Production designer who has run the producing side of the art department: budgets, sourcing, schedules, crews, and client review, on features shot on digital, 16mm, and 35mm, and on brand work for Google and Meta.

1.2Since May 2026, contracted to three AI programs. The work is stress testing generative systems over days to find where they break before users do: misread intent, quality collapse, and failure under the demands of a real production.

1.3The two halves are one skill. A set has to hold up under a camera that sees everything. A generated frame has to hold up under the same eye. Seven years of building the first is what makes the second possible to judge.

3.Built

3.1Ultralight Base. A free reference site for ultralight backpacking. Designed, built, and maintained independently. A base-weight calculator, a database of 1,076 items across 266 brands, a permit calendar, and guides to 60 trails, 40 of them permit-controlled. Works offline once saved to a phone.

3.2The data is kept current by an automated monthly pipeline. It checks permit windows, prices, and weights against official and manufacturer sources, rebuilds the trail pages, tests the site, and submits every change for review before it goes live.

6.Position

6.1Generative AI is a production tool, not a replacement for the people who decide what a film is. Every new tool in film history arrived with the same prediction of the end of the craft. Each time, the people who learned what the tool made possible did the best work with it.

6.2The tools fail in ways that only someone who has made the real thing will notice. That is the work.
