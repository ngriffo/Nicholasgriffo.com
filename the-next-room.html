THE NEXT ROOM

Opening of a launch film for Odyssey-3

Treatment by Nicholas Griffo

Every shot after the first click is generated by the world model in response to the viewer’s action. Nothing is pre-rendered. Notes in brackets say what each beat shows about the model.

FADE IN:

OVER BLACK

Sound only. Rain falls on broad leaves.

EXT. RAINFOREST RUIN - DAWN

First-person view. A 50mm lens, close to human sight. Light falls on a stone doorway. Strangler fig roots climb the stone, as though the door grows out of the rainforest floor.

The view tilts up. Dawn under clouds. Mist. Small movements in the leaves.

A cursor rests at the center of the frame.

ON SCREEN (lower third):
PUSH THE STONE DOOR.

Click. The door begins to move.

[The model: an action goes in and the next frame comes out. The weight of the door and its swing come from physics the model learned, not from an animator.]

HARD CUT TO:

INT. FLOODED HALL - CONTINUOUS

Same lens. The door is still settling, open now. A flooded hall under a broken roof. One shaft of light from above. Water drips and birds call.

ON SCREEN:
STEP INTO THE WATER.

The view moves to the water and looks down. Rings spread across the surface, and the reflection breaks up and re-forms. We hold until it is still, then look up.

A vine falls into view.

[The model: the water answers the step. Ripples, reflection and stillness are dynamics it learned by watching the world.]

ON SCREEN:
PULL THE VINE.

The view looks up. Leaves and water fall through the shaft of light, and the opening in the roof widens. Daylight spreads down the far wall.

Still in first person, the view turns to the wall. Carvings, hidden in the dark a moment ago, are now lit by sunlight glittering through the canopy. The view comes to rest and holds, fixed.

The carvings read:

CARVED IN THE STONE:
NO ONE BUILT THIS WORLD.
IT ANSWERED YOU.

[The model: cause and effect. One pull changes the light, and the light changes what the room shows. Text that holds its shape on a lit surface is one more thing the model has to get right.]

ON SCREEN (lower third):
ODYSSEY
Learn the world to better it.

FADE OUT.
